Mochi's Life: privacy

Mochi's Life is a learning game for young children on Roku TVs. This page says what the app does and does not do with information. Last updated 9 November 2026.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for names, email addresses or any other personal information.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no purchases.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking, and it sends nothing about the child or the TV to us.
  • It has no links out of the app and no chat.

What the app keeps on the TV

The stars a child has earned, how far they have got through Mochi's days, when Mochi's rest time ends, the age and difficulty a grown-up chose, and whether the sound is on. These stay on the TV and are never sent anywhere. Removing the app removes them.

What the app downloads

The game's pictures and voices are downloaded from this website, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub receives the TV's internet address when a file is downloaded; see GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing else is sent.
